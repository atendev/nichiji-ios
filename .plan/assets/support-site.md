# Nichiji

One Kanji. One Day. Every Day.

Nichiji is an iPhone app that teaches one kanji a day, with readings, stroke order, example words and review.

## Support

Email <support email> with questions, bug reports or purchase problems. Include your iPhone model and iOS version.

To restore Nichiji Pro on a new device, open Settings > Nichiji Pro and tap Restore purchases.

[Privacy policy](privacy/)

## privacy/index.md (privacy policy, https://atendev.github.io/nichiji-site/privacy/)

```markdown
# Nichiji privacy policy

Nichiji does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your study progress and settings stay on your device, in the app and its widgets. Apple processes in-app purchases, and Nichiji never sees your payment details. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it. Questions: <support email>.
```

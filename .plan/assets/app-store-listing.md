## Description

One Kanji. One Day. Every Day.

Nichiji gives you one new kanji each day, with its meanings, on and kun readings, stroke order, example words and spoken readings. Practice writing it, and Nichiji brings it back for review until you know it.

TODAY

- Stroke order diagrams and a practice pad with an optional guide.
- Example words from JMdict with readings and meanings.

REVIEW
- Swipe flashcards. A kanji comes back after one day, then three days, and leaves the queue once you know it.
- Pick the card types, the order and the session size.

DICTIONARY
- Browse all 2,383 kanji by JLPT level or school grade.
- Search by kanji, kana, romaji or English.

YOUR PROGRESS
- A day streak that forgives one missed day a week, an activity grid, milestones, progress by level, and the kanji you forget most.
- Your full study history.

REMINDERS AND WIDGETS
- A daily reminder at the time you choose.
- A Home Screen widget that shows today's kanji.

NICHIJI PRO
Nichiji Pro is a one-time purchase. No subscription. It adds:
- Lock Screen widgets
- Export your study history as a CSV file
- Photo lookup: point the camera at a sign to look up its kanji
- Handwriting search: draw a kanji to find it

Everything else is free, with no time limit and no ads.

PRIVACY
No account and no sign-in. Nichiji collects no data, and everything stays on your iPhone.

Dictionary data: KANJIDIC2 / JMdict (EDRDG), stroke order: KanjiVG. Full credits in Settings > About > Sources.

Before upload, cut the photo lookup or handwriting search line if that feature did not ship (Guideline 2.3.1).

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in. All content ships with the app, and it works offline. To reach the Nichiji Pro purchase, open You > Learned / History and tap "Export history", or open the widget guide in Settings and tap "Unlock lock screen widgets". Restore purchases is in Settings > Nichiji Pro.

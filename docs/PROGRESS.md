| Dictionary search | done | A search bar above the browse grid searches all 2,383 kanji and 18,195 words whatever the study pool. The query's script picks the mode (kanji, kana, or Latin as romaji plus English); one `SearchFolding` runs on data and query; Kanji and Words rank by exact/prefix/substring tier, field, frequency and JMdict `rank`. A pure `DictionarySearchIndex` is built in memory on first open and scanned about 150 ms after the last keystroke. A word row opens kanji detail directly or a "Which kanji?" sheet. A Pro "Draw a kanji to search" button opens draw to search (see the Handwriting search row). |
| Handwriting search | done | Pro only; locked, the button opens the paywall. The "Draw to search" sheet has a pad, five matches after each finished stroke, Undo and Clear, and a match fills the search bar. `HandwritingTemplateMatcher` ports the prototype (12 points per stroke, bounding box, Hungarian stroke assignment, templates within 2 strokes) over templates built at runtime from the stroke SVGs, and scores at least 76% top-1 and 89% top-5 on the 104 drawn samples in CI. On device, `MLKitHandwritingModel` (CocoaPods, device-only) takes over once its `ja` model downloads, filtered to shipped kanji, with the matcher as fallback. Before release: the privacy label and privacy copy ("Find what ML Kit sends to Google"), a committed `Podfile.lock`, and an on-device check of ML Kit. |

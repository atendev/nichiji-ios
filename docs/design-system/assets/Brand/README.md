The Nichiji brand marks, all built from a geometric 日 (nichi, "day") so they never depend on a font.

- `nichiji-icon.svg`: the iOS app icon, washi 日 on a full-bleed vermilion square. Supply it unmasked; iOS applies the corner mask. Washi ink is #f4eee2 on vermilion #b8321d.
- `nichiji-icon-dark.svg`: the dark appearance, vermilion #e8603f 日 on sumi #15130f.
- `nichiji-mark.svg`: the hanko seal, a rounded vermilion square with a knocked-out washi 日 and transparent corners. Use it in the app (about screen, "Got it" stamp, onboarding) at 24px or larger.

These are images shown through `<img>`, so their inks are fixed: do not recolor them or place the seal on vermilion. Keep clear space equal to a quarter of the mark's width.
